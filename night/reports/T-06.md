# T-06 — Farm view redesign - terrain on one side, all cards in a column
Status: done
## What changed
- **Layout (`FarmView.tsx`, `explorer.css`)**: the farm view is now an information column next to the 3D terrain.
  - Desktop (≥ 1100 px): fixed column on the left, `--fx-col: clamp(420px, 32vw, 480px)` (461 px at 1440, 420 px at 1280). Top to bottom it holds the farm header (eyebrow, ha with the current crop, SIGPAC line, warnings), the four indicator cards stacked in the order 1 Water, 2 Soil, 3 Market, 4 Climate risk, the timeline with the rotation selector and Priority, and a small-print note (relief exaggeration and disclaimer). The column scrolls if the screen is very short.
  - Tablet (760–1099 px): the same layout with a narrower column, `clamp(330px, 36vw, 390px)` (369 px at 1024).
  - Phone (< 760 px): terrain on top (40 % of the height), column below (60 %, scrollable, cards in a 2×2 grid).
  - A tighter variant for screens up to 800 px high makes 1280×720 and 1024×768 fit with no scrolling of the panels.
  - Cards are now a compact grid: number badge, icon, label, score and word on the first row; bar and "± vs. current" on the second; evidence text on the third (the soil card also shows the texture strip).
  - Over the terrain: Back (top-left of the terrain area), the satellite/model toggle, relief ×1/×2/×3.5, re-centre and "Full data" (top-right), breadcrumbs (bottom-left), crop legend and offline badge (bottom-right). The director panel moves under the tools at level 3.
  - "Full data" opens as a drawer exactly over the column; on the phone it covers the lower 60 %.
- **Numbered markers (`diorama.ts`)**: the SVG leader lines and the "xx ha" pin are gone. `attachOverlay({ markers, onHover, onSelect })` creates four markers. Each is a coloured dot on the exact terrain point, a stem and a numbered badge. The points are: water = lowest point of the farm, soil = soil profile on the wall facing the camera, market = centre, risk = highest point. They are re-placed every rendered frame, so they stay attached while orbiting, and they hide when their point is behind the block. When two points are close, the badge moves to the first free slot around its dot (slanted stem), so badges never cover each other.
  - Hovering or focusing a card (cards are focusable) highlights its marker (`dio.highlight(k)`).
  - Hovering a marker highlights its card; clicking a marker scrolls to its card and focuses it.
  - The number badge on each card has a tooltip saying where its marker is.
- **Framing (`diorama.ts`)**: `framePadding()` (now public) reads the free area from an invisible `.dio-frame` element. CSS places that element with the same breakpoints as the column, so layout and camera cannot disagree.
  - `isoView()` now projects the 8 corners of the raised block with a scratch camera, scales the distance until the block fills 94 % of the free area, and sets the view offset so its centre is the free area's centre.
  - The orbit `maxDistance` is widened when the fitted view needs it (narrow phones).
  - After a real resize (> 40 px, e.g. rotating a tablet) the view is re-framed once it settles.
- **Hand-off (`controller.ts`)**: at level 3 the map uses `dio.framePadding()` as its padding. A new `farmCamera(tile)` does the 0.75 zoom-out around the free-area centre instead of the screen centre. The map therefore hands the tile over exactly where the block will stand, and the block rises in place instead of sliding sideways. Exit uses the same camera. The canvas is still full-screen, so `tileRect` and the snapshot are unchanged.
- Small text change: the market evidence now ends in "price proxy, no costs" (it was "…, excluding costs") so it fits on one line in the column.
- **Attempt 2: e2e (`app/e2e/layout.spec.ts`)**:
  - The three `KNOWN` / `test.fail` entries (`mobile/level3`, `mobile/level3-ronda`, `mobile/level3-full-data`) are removed. This redesign fixed the Back-over-header overlap on phones, so those tests are now ordinary passing tests and `KNOWN` is empty.
  - The layout suite now runs every scenario at four sizes: `LAYOUT_VIEWPORTS` = the shared `VIEWPORTS` (desktop 1440×900, mobile 390×844) plus `laptop` 1280×720 and `tablet` 1024×768. The shared `VIEWPORTS` in `helpers.ts` is unchanged, so the a11y suite still runs at desktop and phone only.
- **Attempt 2: disclaimer placement (`explorer.css`)**. The new 1024×768 check found a real overlap that predates this ticket: at level 2 the breadcrumb ("Andalucía / Málaga / Antequera") overlapped the centred disclaimer by 5×22 px. Measuring with the longest municipality name (Villanueva de la Concepción, breadcrumb ends at x = 399) showed it would overlap at 1280 px as well. The fix changes the layout; no check was loosened:
  - ≥ 1100 px: the disclaimer stays centred, with `max-width: min(620px, 100vw - 840px)`, so both edges always clear the breadcrumb and Back.
  - 760–1099 px: it sits left-aligned at the free bottom of the narrative column (left 34 px, 72 px from the bottom, 250 px wide, 3 lines), above the breadcrumb. While a toast is visible it fades out (`.xp:has(.toast.visible)`): the toast is temporary and on top, and at narrow tablet widths it would otherwise cover the disclaimer.
  - Phones: unchanged (hidden, as before).
