# T-07 — Farm view - surface what really matters for this farm
Status: done
## What changed
- **`app/src/explorer/insights.ts`** (new, pure): `farmInsights(facts)` returns 1–3 ranked insights `{ id, severity: critical | warning | good, headline (≤ 60 chars), detail (one sentence with the number), target: card | 'rotations' | null }`. It also has `recommendation(scores, profile)`, one sentence on the best rotation and why, and `bestRotation`. The rules table is at the top of the file. Thresholds come from the calculator or the pipeline:
  - **Slope**: warning from 12 % (candidate rule `pendiente_max`, `pipeline/build.py`); critical from 30 % or `apt = "pendiente"` (`PENDIENTE_FUERTE`, `export_app_fincas.py`).
  - **Water**: warning when R0's P20 water score is < 40 or the deficit is ≥ 720 mm/yr (80 % of the calibrated 900 mm reference). Critical when R0 hits the P20 < 20 blocker or the deficit is ≥ 900 mm.
  - **Heat**: warning from 10 hot days in May–June (≥ 32 °C), where heat alone removes ≥ 25 % of the risk score (`1 − days/40`). Critical above 25 days (calculator warning).
  - **Frost**: warning above 5 days (calculator).
  - **Soil water**: warning when available water is ≤ 100 mm (the calculator gives 0 storage points there).
  - **Land use**: warning when `apt` is monte, roca or another non-slope code, quoting the WorldCover %.
  - **Permanent crop**: warning when cover is `arbolado` or ≥ 4 seen seasons are `P`.
  - **No data**: warning when the farm has no scores.
  - **Best option** (good): "Best option: R1 (+2.8 points over current)" or "Current rotation is already the best option", for the chosen priority.
  - **Ranking**: critical → warning → good. Within a severity, land-use, permanent and no-data come first; then whichever insight is furthest past its threshold (value ÷ threshold).
- **Measured on real data**: almost no Málaga farm reaches the calculator's own warning levels (heat > 25 days, frost > 5, P20 water < 40), so insights built only on those would be empty. That is why the heat and deficit thresholds are taken from the score formulas. Sample of 23k farms: every one gets 1–3 insights; the most common sets are permanent/slope/water, slope + water + best, and best only.
- **`FarmView.tsx` / `explorer.css`**:
  - **Insights block**: first element of the column.
    - The top insight has a severity label with icon and text (Critical / Warning / Opportunity), so colour is not the only cue. Its headline is the largest text in the column (Fraunces 27 px desktop, 23 tablet, 22 short screens, 21 phone), followed by its sentence.
    - The other insights are one-line `<details>` disclosures. Opening one shows its sentence and emphasises its card.
    - Hovering an insight highlights its card and terrain marker.
    - To keep the top insight the largest text, the farm title (ha) went from 40 to 24 px and card scores from 28 to 24 px.
  - **Emphasis**:
    - The card behind the top insight is emphasised (coloured border, tint, left bar) and keeps its evidence.
    - The other cards recede to one row: label · score · delta, with a thin bar along the bottom edge. Their evidence stays in the DOM, visually hidden but read by screen readers, and is shown as a tooltip.
    - Clicking a card (or Enter/Space on it), or clicking its terrain marker, moves the emphasis to that card.
    - If the top insight is about the rotations (best option, land use, permanent crop), the rotation dock is emphasised and all four cards recede.
  - **Screen readers**: an `aria-live="polite"` region is in the page empty and filled 700 ms after the farm view opens: "Key point for this farm: <headline>. Also: <others>." It is not re-announced when the priority changes.
  - **Recommendation**: a line under the rotation selector, e.g. "R1 (+ Chickpea) is best for Water: 67.0 vs. 64.2 now, mainly better soil (+7.5) despite a weaker market (−4.8)." or "Keep the current rotation: it is best for Market (68.6), ahead of R1 (67.4)."
  - **Header**: the old apt / permanent-crop warning lines are removed; the insights now say this with numbers.
  - **Units**: non-breaking spaces between numbers and units ("53.7 %"), so they never wrap apart.

  - `app/src/explorer/insights.test.ts`: 21 Vitest tests.
    - Real farms: Antequera 2900059683, Ronda 2900263939, apt farms 2900063712 (`pendiente`) and 2900063216 (`monte`).
    - Every threshold edge.
    - Ranking, the 60-character headline limit, and that every sentence carries a number.
    - Recommendation texts.
  - e2e:
    - Four new `flows.spec.ts` tests (Antequera, Ronda, apt farm, a calm farm). They cover: top headline text, the top insight being the largest font in the column, emphasised/receded cards, live-region text and no re-announcement, the recommendation for two priorities, and that opening an insight or clicking a card moves the emphasis.
    - Two new layout scenarios (`level3-steep`, `level3-calm`) at all four sizes.
    - New farms in `helpers.ts`: `FARMS.steep`, `FARMS.calm`.
- **Layout checker (`e2e/layout.ts`), made scroll-aware, not loosened**:
  - The insights make the column taller than a phone's 60 % sheet (and than 1280×720 for long-headline farms). T-06 designed that column to scroll (`overflow-y: auto`), but the checker reported anything below the fold as "outside the viewport".
  - Now a panel inside a scrolling box is measured vertically by that box, and that box must still be inside the viewport. Horizontally nothing changed.
  - Overlaps are computed on the part of each panel that is actually shown, clipped by its scrolling ancestors.
  - The self-test (`layout-checker.spec.ts`) plants a reachable below-the-fold panel (must not be reported) and a scrolling box that itself sticks out (must be reported).
  - `layout.spec.ts` now also scrolls the farm column to the end and re-checks overlaps, outside and clipping, so the content that scrolls into view is covered.
