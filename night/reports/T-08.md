# T-08 — Accessible colour system - fewer colours, enough contrast, never colour alone
Status: done
## What changed
Summary: the explorer now has **one** token palette (CSS custom properties in `explorer.css`, mirrored in `tokens.ts` for three.js and MapLibre) and every colour in `app/src/explorer` comes from it. Three rules are enforced by unit tests (`tokens.test.ts`): the CSS and TS tokens are identical, there are no stray colour literals, and every text/UI pair meets WCAG AA. Real rendered contrast is measured in e2e. Every colour-coded meaning also has a word, an icon, a letter or a pattern.

### 2. Colour-blind check (computed, not by eye)
Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes 2009 matrices (severity 1.0 = full protanopia / deuteranopia / tritanopia), applied in linear RGB, then CIEDE2000 distance (≈ 2 just noticeable, ≥ 10 clearly different). Code: `colorMath.ts`; enforced in `tokens.test.ts`.

| Vision | good | warn | bad | accent | ΔE good/warn | ΔE good/bad | ΔE warn/bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| normal | #6DB6FF | #F0983A | #FF6FA0 | #E9D58B | 48.5 | 45.4 | 39.7 |
| protanopia | #97B9FF | #B4A02D | #888FA1 | #E3D285 | 51.7 | **18.2** | 36.3 |
| deuteranopia | #84ABFE | #C8B43B | #AEAA9D | #E9D88E | 54.9 | 30.7 | **21.5** |
| tritanopia (rare) | #00C7D0 | #FF8485 | #FF6282 | #F6CAC2 | 55.1 | 74.5 | **8.5** |

- Thresholds in the test: ≥ 15 for protanopia and deuteranopia, ≥ 8 for tritanopia (orange vs. pink is the closest pair there; the word + icon cover it).
- Accent vs. each status colour: ΔE ≥ 11.5 in every vision (min: accent/warn deuteranopia 11.5).
- **Before** (old green `#B9D27A` / amber `#E8B86A` / red `#E58C84`): good vs. warning ΔE **4.1** (protanopia) and **2.9** (deuteranopia): the "good" and "warning" colours were practically the same.
- Crops, new four colours: min ΔE 19.6 (normal), 14.0 (protanopia), 15.7 (deuteranopia). **Before** (ten colours): 1.5 (trigo vs. winter cereal) in normal vision.

### 5. Crop colours: fewer
Ten crop classes → **four colours**, shown at most at once: arable (wheat, sunflower, winter cereal, summer crop, mixed), legume (chickpea, vetch), fallow (fallow, fallow or pasture), permanent. Land cover of the neighbouring fields went from 6 colours to 4 (`COVER_TOKENS`: natural, trees = the permanent-crop colour, built/bare, water). `CROPS` in `scene.ts` now carries `letter`, `group`, `ink` (text colour that reads on its fill) and `color` (from the tokens); `cropInfo()` replaces the scattered fallbacks (`#777`, `#666`, `#A3835F`).

The 3D materials are scene art, not UI: they carry no text and no meaning |
| `clouds.ts` | 4 shader constants | unchanged, marked as exceptions |
| `FarmView.tsx` | 5 hex (soil texture ×3, crop fallbacks) | 0: `SOIL_TEXTURE`, `cropInfo()` |

**Justified exceptions** (each carries a `colour-ok:` comment, checked by the test):
- `clouds.ts` ×4: cloud shader uniforms (white / grey / warm veil). A cloud is white; not a UI colour.
- `diorama.ts`: `MASK` / `maskAlpha()` (white = "on" in alpha masks that only feed the shaders), and the procedural soil speckle `rgba(...)` that varies randomly around `SCENE.ground`.
- `controller.ts`: `rgba(0,0,0,0)`, the transparent end of a line gradient.
- Satellite imagery and the 3D model's lighting are not colour-coded information.
